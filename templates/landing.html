HONEST BY DESIGN

Know what the data can—and cannot—tell you

Historical dataset readings are not live field telemetry. JalSaathi does not infer soil moisture, crop stages, irrigation timing, or water duration without verified inputs and a connected model. Weather depends on provider availability and is labeled when cached.
